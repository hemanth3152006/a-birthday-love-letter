# Birthday love-letter page

## Goal
Create a polished, personal-feeling birthday webpage that celebrates her and gently expresses a one-sided love without asking for anything in return.

## Build
- Replace the template home page with a full birthday experience: a memorable opening, a revealable love letter, and a warm closing wish.
- Use a distinctive, cinematic romantic direction with a custom birthday visual, refined type, and mobile-friendly layout.
- Keep all copy respectful and easy to personalize with her name once supplied.
- Give the reveal interaction a clear open/close state and accessible controls.
- Add unique page metadata and keep the experience frontend-only.

## Technical details
Use the existing TanStack home route and global CSS design tokens; avoid adding backend services or dependencies. Verify the reveal interaction in the browser and run the existing routing test.
